CASTING CALL – UPCOMING HINDI FEATURE FILM

Yash Raj Films is looking for fresh female talent for an upcoming feature film. Women aged 20–25 years with strong screen presence, natural expressions and a passion for acting are invited to audition. Freshers are welcome. The shoot will take place in Mumbai.
